Food safety

Before donating, every provider is asked to confirm the following.

  • Provide accurate preparation time
  • Provide accurate expiry / use-by information
  • Describe ingredients where possible
  • Mention known allergens
  • Maintain appropriate storage
  • Do not donate visibly unsafe food
Food Safety Notice: Providers are responsible for accurately reporting food preparation, storage, and safety information. BachekoKhana does not represent food as safe solely because it has been listed.
