About BachekoKhana

Bacheko khana, kasailai sahara.

BachekoKhana is a digital platform designed to make food redistribution easier, more organized and more dignified. Instead of letting surplus food disappear, providers can list it and people can discover, request and receive it through one connected system.

Surplus food is not just waste. With the right connection, it can become someone's next meal.
